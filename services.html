Services

Our services are primarily geared towards creating customized cyber tools and environments for engineering and medical organizations.

We can design and develop new customized cyber tools or cyber-physical tools for a variety of process domains and contexts using AI and XR technologies (VR/AR/MR).

We can create cyber tools and digital twins for various domains (manufacturing, medicine, space systems and cyber learning)

These include

  • advanced manufacturing and robotics (cognitive and smart manufacturing, industry 4.0/5.0)
  • medicine and health care (smart health)
  • space systems (near and deep space mission to Moon and Mars).

We also can build learning tools to help autistic students learn science and engineering topics. We will be glad to work with K-12 programs and schools.

Consulting

Technical consulting for XR (VR/AR/MR), digital twins, and cyber-human-physical systems across manufacturing, medicine, space, and cyber learning.

We help teams explore simulation opportunities, model complex workflows, and plan tools for analysis, design, and decision-making.

Prototype Tools Development

We design and develop custom interactive prototypes using XR, digital twins, and AI to simulate and evaluate cyber-physical processes.

Applications span manufacturing and robotics, surgical simulation, space mission scenarios, and cyber learning environments.

Benefits of our services

Our next generation tools and consulting services are unique and provide a set of benefits to enable your enterprise to accomplish the following

  • reduce associated cost and time
  • help identify problems in a product design idea and/or the processes involved in manufacturing your product
  • our simulation solutions help users become more proficient in a target procedure or method in significantly reduced time (surgery or manufacturing, among others)
  • capable of providing user feedback on products already developed by others or we can develop HCI oriented tools which improve usability, reduce cognitive load and improves user experience
  • substantial cost reduction in the development of a new process

Our engineering team

Our engineering team is composed of pioneers and global leaders in creating XR based digital twins for various domains, simulating cyber human physical interactions to support:

  • digital and cognitive manufacturing
  • Smart health and other domains.

Our team has experience creating such advanced tools and environments for manufacturing, surgical training and Near/Deep Space Missions
