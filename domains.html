Advanced Manufacturing & Robotics

We specialize in the design of Extended Reality (XR) based environments encompassing Virtual Reality (VR) and Mixed Reality (MR) as well build cyber-physical environments linking the XR environment to a physical environment

Our experts pioneered the notion of Virtual Prototypes, way before the current concept of Digital Twins became popular (for the record, they mean the same).

For our customers, we can design and build specific XR based environments at various levels of abstraction (at the machine level, the work cell level or the entire factory)

We can build such environments based on customer preferences of the VR platforms (Vive, Meta Quest and others)

Our team was the first to demonstrate the new Digital Twin approach which allow engineers to propose and compare the feasibility of alternative manufacturing or robotic assembly plans ideas, before any robotic or manufacturing cell for a specific application was built

We called this virtual manufacturing because it allowed engineers to virtually design a robotic work cell or manufacturing task and identify drawbacks and problems, compare various process details and make needed modifications before purchasing or building any robotic or manufacturing capability.

Our mantra was design, simulate, modify, validate using such Virtual Prototypes or Digital Twins

We set the standard for what needs to be simulated inside a virtual prototype or digital twin and how to accomplish this. Our interdisciplinary teams had expertise in robotics / manufacturing (typically mechanical /industrial engineers) and VR model building (industrial engineers and computer scientists)

We subsequently pioneered a new class of cyber-physical tools which allowed engineers to propose ideas in VR environments and after analyzing and validating them, subsequently interact with corresponding physical twins comprising of robots and manufacturing equipment and assemble a given product.

Our information model driven simulators allowed manufacturing organizations to perceive and understand complex manufacturing and robotic interactions, model them and use them as a basis to mimic engineering decision making. We are the group that put the ‘smart’ in smart manufacturing.

Today, we continue this legacy of information centric cyber-physical tools and are now pioneering the next generation of cognitive and AI based manufacturing tools

Smart Health & Medicine

We began working with surgeons, nurses and residents in the 2000s and developed the first cyber-physical framework to allow surgeons to train residents at distributed locations using XR tools

We specialize in designing and building simulators for orthopedic surgery; our design team includes expert orthopedic surgeons who are leaders in hand and leg surgery

Our participatory design approach is unique and allows us to build advanced simulators with a high level of fidelity

Space Systems

Our experience team has worked on NASA related projects involving Moon and Mars missions

XR based simulators are key to preparing astronauts for our Nation’s return to the Moon

We also have experience working on deep space habitat projects as part of NASA’s Xhab initiatives

Cyber Learning

Our team’s prior experience includes working with students, parents and school teachers to develop specialized learning environments for autistic students
